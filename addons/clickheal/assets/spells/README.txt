Optional spell icons for the hover flyout (setup panel > Flyout > Show spell icons).
Name each PNG after the spell key: cure4.png, curaga2.png, raise3.png, haste.png ...
Buffs and single-debuff cleanses (Haste, Poisona...) already use the game's status icons; files here take priority.
Use art you have the right to use. Square images (32x32 or 64x64) work best.
