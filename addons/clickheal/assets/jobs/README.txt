Put job icons here as PNG files named by the three-letter job (WHM.png, BLM.png, RDM.png, ...).
When "Job icons instead of text" is on, ClickHeal draws these; any job without a file falls back to
the built-in pictogram. Square images (32x32 or 64x64) work best.
Use art you have the right to use, for example icons you extract from your own FFXI install.
